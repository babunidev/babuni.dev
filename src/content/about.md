Online, I'm known as **Babuni**. Behind that name is **Alex**, with interests stretching across practical work, art, music, games, technology and imagined worlds. No single title quite covers it all.

## The person behind the projects

Originally from Germany and now living in Sweden, I have spent years doing work far beyond the computer screen. Baking, food service, warehouse work, gardening and outdoor maintenance have all been part of my life. Working with trees and hedges, handling tools, caring for a place and making something tangible bring their own kind of satisfaction.

Alongside that practical side, I've always been drawn to stories, games, drawing, music and the freedom to create something from scratch. Over the years, I've written and performed songs, developed characters, imagined worlds and played games not only for their challenges, but for the people and stories that grow around them. The World of Warcraft guild ROAR is one expression of that social and imaginative side.

Curiosity tends to carry me between these interests. A game can inspire a useful tool; a character can grow into a story; a musical idea can become a recording; a technical problem can become a creative experiment. Learning by doing, changing direction and discovering unexpected solutions are all part of what keeps me creating.

## Babuni and babuni.dev

**Babuni** is my wider creative identity. **Babuni Gaming**, **Babuni Dev**, music, art and worldbuilding are different branches of the same life and imagination.

**babuni.dev** is my development portfolio and a project in its own right. Its shifting, centerless grid gathers finished work, early experiments and the stories of how they came to be. Exploration matters more here than following a prescribed route.

## What takes shape here

Among my larger undertakings is **Morkahja**, an online game that people can actually play, with its own website and a growing world behind it. **Ethera** is another ambitious game idea, but still at a very early stage of development. Their scope and maturity are different, and neither should be mistaken for a finished project simply because it appears here.

World of Warcraft addons make up much of the project collection by number. Smaller creations such as RoarForever, SocialForever and FlourishForever grow out of everyday play and ideas for making the game more expressive or useful. Other work includes the Button Game, music, storytelling, visual experiments such as Anskar Grotzschnapf, and experiments with local software and AI.

Rather than writing the code myself, I focus on imagining, designing and directing what should be made. AI helps with programming and implementation, while the creative direction, decisions and evaluation remain mine. The development records aim to make that collaboration visible, including the ideas, experiments, revisions and results.
