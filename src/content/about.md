Online, I'm known as **Babuni**. Behind that name is **Alex**, with interests in practical work, art, music, games, technology and imagined worlds.

## The person behind the projects

![Illustrated portrait of Babuni](/babuni-about-portrait.webp)

Originally from Germany and now living in Sweden, I've worked in baking, food service, warehouses, gardening and outdoor maintenance. 
Alongside that practical side, I've always been drawn to stories, games, drawing, music and creating things from scratch. Over the years, I've written and performed songs, developed characters, imagined worlds and enjoyed the people and stories that grow around games.

Curiosity tends to carry me between these interests. A game can inspire a useful tool; a character can grow into a story; a musical idea can become a recording; a technical problem can become a creative experiment. Learning by doing, changing direction and finding solutions are all part of my work.

## About babuni.dev

**babuni.dev** is my development portfolio and a project of its own. Its shifting, centerless grid gathers finished work, early experiments and the stories of how they came to be. Visitors are free to explore it in their own way.

## What takes shape here

**Morkahja** is an online game that people can play, with its own website and a world I've been developing. **Ethera** is another game project, still at a very early stage.

World of Warcraft addons make up a large number of the smaller projects here. RoarForever, SocialForever and FlourishForever, for example, started with ideas from playing the game. Other work includes the Button Game, music, storytelling, visual experiments such as Anskar Grotzschnapf, and local software and AI projects.

For development work, my role is to come up with ideas, design systems, make decisions and direct their implementation. AI assists with programming and technical work. The development records document that collaboration, including changes, experiments and results.
