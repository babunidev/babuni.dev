## Specification boundaries

The core creative direction is consistently represented: a draggable recycled stone-tile field, project identity separate from rendered instances, informational portal bubbles, cell-reserving small bloom, focused full bloom, and conventional detailed readers. Svelte, exact grid algorithms, collision handling, buffer size, and performance claims remain proposals or open questions.

An initial center-out reveal temporarily leaves cells unfilled, while visual acceptance rules require no blank margins during drag, shifts, and resize. Before interaction implementation, define when that initial reveal ends and panning begins. This is an AI-identified specification question, not a confirmed defect or an approved design change.
